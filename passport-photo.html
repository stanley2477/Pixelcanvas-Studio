About the Passport Photo Maker

PixelCanvas Studio's Passport Photo Maker helps prepare a portrait for passport-style and identity-photo applications. It provides cropping, resizing, positioning, background adjustment and printable-sheet options in one workflow.

How to use the Passport Photo Maker

  1. Take a photo with your camera or select an existing image.
  2. Choose a target photo dimension or enter custom dimensions.
  3. Adjust the crop, zoom and position so the subject is framed appropriately.
  4. Choose a suitable background color.
  5. Create the photo and review the preview.
  6. Optionally create a print sheet with multiple copies.
  7. Download the final image.

Passport photo dimensions

Passport and identity-photo dimensions are not universal. Different countries, authorities and application systems can require different physical dimensions, pixel dimensions, background rules, head positioning and image specifications. The presets on this tool are starting points, not a guarantee that a photo meets the requirements of a particular authority.

Important:

Always check the current official requirements for the country or authority receiving your application before submitting a photo. A correctly sized image can still be rejected if it does not meet other requirements such as facial position, expression, lighting, background, image quality or digital-file rules.

Preparing a better passport-style photo

Use even lighting, face the camera directly, keep the head naturally positioned and avoid strong shadows or distracting backgrounds. Make sure the entire face is visible and follow any official rules about glasses, head coverings, clothing and expression that apply to your application.

Print-sheet layout

The print-sheet option places multiple copies of the prepared photo onto a printable page area. Printer margins, paper size and physical print scaling can affect the final result, so verify the printed dimensions with a ruler before using the sheet for an application.

Tip:

Keep the original photograph. If an authority requires a different size or crop later, having the original gives you more flexibility.

Privacy and file handling
