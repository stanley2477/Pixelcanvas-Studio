Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PixelCanvas Studio approaches information, files, analytics, advertising and third-party services when you use the website.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Overview

PixelCanvas Studio provides browser-based tools for working with images, PDFs and documents. We aim to minimize unnecessary collection of information and to provide clear information about how the website and its tools operate.

2. Files you process

When you select an image or document in a tool, the file is made available to that tool so the requested operation can be performed. Where a tool is implemented for browser-side processing, the file can be processed locally on your device without being uploaded to a PixelCanvas Studio server.

Processing behavior can vary by tool and may depend on third-party libraries or services used by that tool. The relevant tool page should be checked for its specific processing information.

3. Temporary processing and deletion

PixelCanvas Studio does not intend to retain users' uploaded files longer than necessary for a tool's operation. If a particular feature requires server-side processing or temporary storage, the applicable implementation may handle files differently. Users should not upload highly sensitive material unless they understand and accept the processing method of the feature they are using.

4. Information you provide

If you contact PixelCanvas Studio by email, we may receive the information contained in your message, including your email address and any information you choose to provide. This information may be used to respond to your request, investigate reported problems and provide support.

5. Automatically collected information

Like many websites, PixelCanvas Studio may receive limited technical or usage information through website infrastructure, analytics tools and security systems. Depending on the services enabled, this may include information such as browser type, device information, approximate geographic information, pages visited, referral information and usage events.

6. Analytics

PixelCanvas Studio may use analytics services to understand website traffic, tool usage and performance. Analytics information helps us identify problems, understand which pages are useful and improve the website. Analytics providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The website or third-party services used on it may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for functions such as preferences, analytics, security and advertising. Your browser provides controls for managing or blocking cookies, although doing so may affect some website functionality.

8. Advertising

PixelCanvas Studio may display advertising through third-party advertising providers. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalize advertising. The exact providers and technologies used may change as the website develops.

9. Third-party services

Some website features may rely on third-party libraries, APIs, hosting providers, analytics services, advertising providers or other infrastructure. These services may process technical information or data required for the feature to operate. Their own terms and privacy policies may apply.

10. Links to other websites

PixelCanvas Studio may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of websites that we do not operate. Review the privacy policy of an external service before providing information to it.

11. Children's privacy

PixelCanvas Studio is a general-purpose website and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

12. Data security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the website and its services to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse or loss. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Your choices

  • You can choose whether to use a particular tool.
  • You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings.
  • You can choose what information to include when contacting us.
  • You can stop using the website at any time.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, tools, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, contact PixelCanvas Studio at contact@pixelcanvasstudio.com.

Important: This page describes the intended privacy approach for PixelCanvas Studio. Before launch, it should be reviewed against the exact analytics, advertising, APIs, libraries, hosting configuration and file-processing architecture actually enabled on the website.
